Outreach Ministry

The Go International Outreach Ministry is a Christ-centered community built on faith, love, and purpose. We began as a small fellowship with a strong passion to reach lives and share the Gospel, and by God’s grace we have grown into a vibrant and expanding family of believers. Our mission is to impact communities both locally and internationally through outreach, discipleship, and the teaching of God’s Word.
