Our Story

All About The Go International Outreach Ministry

The Go International Outreach Ministry (GIOM), known as The Go Church, began as a divine assignment entrusted by the Holy Spirit to Mr. Timothy Williams (Pastor Tims) and supported by his wife, Mrs. Sesinam Dorv-Williams (Lady Sesy). The ministry traces its origin to a season of deep spiritual hunger shortly after their marriage on 18th November 2023.

In January 2024, the Holy Spirit instructed Tims to embark on a 60-day fast. With faith and obedience—and with the steadfast support of his wife—he began the fasting journey on 15th January 2024. During this period of consecration, the Lord started stirring an unusual passion for souls and a burden to carry the message of Jesus to the world.

A defining moment came on 23rd March 2024. After returning from The Art of Evangelism Conference in Kumasi, Pastor Tims was awakened by the Holy Spirit at dawn. The Spirit instructed him: "Take your Bible and your notebook and sit at your study table." In obedience, he sat, opened the Scriptures, and was divinely directed to Matthew 28:18–20, the Great Commission. In that sacred moment, the Holy Spirit commanded him to write the name:

"The Go International Outreach Ministry (GIOM) also known as The Go Church."

With the name came a clear heavenly mandate: to preach the gospel, demonstrate the power of the Holy Spirit, and win five hundred million souls (500,000,000) into the Kingdom of God. This marked the spiritual birth of GIOM—not as a man-made initiative, but as a divine assignment birthed in prayer, fasting, Scripture, and obedience to the Holy Spirit.

From that point, the vision began taking shape through evangelistic efforts, prayer gatherings, and outreach ideas inspired by the ministry patterns of Jesus as seen in:

  • Matthew 4:23–25 — Jesus teaching, preaching, and healing
  • Acts 10:38 — Jesus doing good and healing the oppressed
  • Acts 1:8 — Empowerment to witness
  • Luke 4:18–19 — The mission of the Spirit-filled believer

On 16th March, 2025, under the leading of the Holy Spirit, Pastor Tims started a new branch of The Go Church in Koforidua. The church began humbly in a hostel room, where a small but passionate group of 13 people gathered with a shared hunger to experience God and see lives transformed. One year later, the Lord has graciously increased us and continues to expand His work among us. What started as a small gathering has steadily grown, and God has blessed us with a bigger and much better space where we can gather, fellowship, and enjoy His presence together.

Though the ministry is at its foundational stage, GIOM is built with a long-term vision, structure, and administrative wisdom inspired first and foremost by the Holy Spirit, while also drawing valuable extracts from the constitutions and organizational excellence of The Church of Pentecost, Methodist Church, Lighthouse Chapel International, Winners Chapel International, and the Catholic Canonical Order.

Today, The Go International Outreach Ministry stands in its early stages—growing, building systems, and laying strong foundations but carrying a clear, unshakable mandate from God. GIOM exists to proclaim one message to all who will hear:

"Go... for the nations are waiting for Jesus."

Join us for a life changing encounter with the Word of God, Worship and the Holy Spirit. A Sunday at The Go Church is more than just a church service, it's an experience!
